name: Whyhello logo counters
description: Non-obvious transparency and edge problems when adapting the brand logo for dark backgrounds.
---

The original full Whyhello image has opaque white pixels inside letter counters and a pale matte blended into the edges of the colorful W. When adapting it for a dark background, do not simply recolor the navy letters white or copy the W unchanged; make the letter interiors transparent and remove the light edge matte from the W while retaining antialiasing.

**Why:** White counters blend into light backgrounds but appear as white-filled holes on dark ones, while the W matte makes its edges look jagged and outlined.

**How to apply:** For future logo variants, inspect the source image at actual display size and enlarged over both light and dark backgrounds. Verify that letter openings show the actual background color and the W edges have no pale halo.
