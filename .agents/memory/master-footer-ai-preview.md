---
name: Master footer AI preview
description: The agreed scope for the global utility footer and its first AI chat window.
---

The master utility footer belongs alongside the app shell's persistent navigation. The first assistant window is a local-only preview; it must clearly state that AI replies are not connected, and it must not call a model or persist chat messages until the user asks to connect it.

**Why:** The user asked to establish the chat experience first and explicitly deferred functional AI.

**How to apply:** Keep shared footer behavior in the app shell. Treat any future AI integration or additional footer tools as separately requested scope.
